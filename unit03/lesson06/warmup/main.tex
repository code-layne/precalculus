\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=12pt]

\item Multiply, and write the result in standard form: \quad $(x - 2)(x^2 + 3x - 1)$

\begin{work}
  (x - 2)(x^2 + 3x - 1) &= x^3 + 3x^2 - x - 2x^2 - 6x + 2 \\
                        &= x^3 + x^2 - 7x + 2
\end{work}

Standard form: \blank{4.2cm}

\item Multiply: \quad $(2 + i)(2 - i)$

\begin{work}
  (2 + i)(2 - i) &= 4 - 2i + 2i - i^2 \\
                 &= 4 - (-1) \\
                 &= 5
\end{work}

Product: \blank{1.2cm} \qquad Is the product a real number? \blank{1.2cm}

\item \textit{(Lesson 3.5)} Use the Factor Theorem: is $(x - 2)$ a factor of
$p(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 4$?

\begin{work}
  p(2) &= 2^3 - 3(2)^2 + 4 \\
       &= 8 - 12 + 4 \\
       &= 0
\end{work}

Is $(x - 2)$ a factor? \blank{4.2cm}
