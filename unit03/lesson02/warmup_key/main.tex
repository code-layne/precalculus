\item Evaluate each power. No calculator.

\vspace{4pt}
(a) $(-10)^2 = $ \ans{$100$} \qquad (b) $(-10)^3 = $ \ans{$-1000$} \qquad
(c) $(-10)^4 = $ \ans{$10{,}000$}

\vspace{6pt}
A negative number raised to an \textbf{even} power is \ans{positive}; raised to an
\textbf{odd} power it is \ans{negative}.

\item Multiply and simplify: \quad $(x + 2)(3x - 1)$

\begin{work}
  (x + 2)(3x - 1) &= 3x^2 - x + 6x - 2 \\
                  &= 3x^2 + 5x - 2
\end{work}

Product: \ans{$3x^2 + 5x - 2$} \qquad The term with the highest power: \ans{$3x^2$}
