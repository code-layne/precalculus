\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]

        the numerator and denominator and identifying the quotient of leading terms.
  \item I can write the end behavior of a rational function using limit notation and identify
        horizontal or slant asymptotes from the rational expression.
\end{enumerate}
