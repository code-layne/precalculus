\textbf{1.} A function $f$ has the following values:
$f(2) = 10$, \quad $f(5) = 25$, \quad $f(5.1) = 25.3$.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=6pt]
    \item Compute the average rate of change (AROC) of $f$ from $x = 2$ to $x = 5$.
          Show your work.
    \[
        \text{AROC} = \frac{f(\hphantom{0}) - f(\hphantom{0})}{\hphantom{0} - \hphantom{0}}
        = \frac{\hphantom{000} - \hphantom{000}}{\hphantom{000} - \hphantom{000}}
        = \hphantom{000}
    \]

    \item Use the values at $x = 5$ and $x = 5.1$ to approximate the rate of change of
          $f$ \textbf{at} $x = 5$.
    \[
        \text{AROC on } [5,\;5.1] = \frac{\hphantom{000} - \hphantom{000}}{\hphantom{000}}
        = \hphantom{000}
    \]
    The rate of change at $x = 5$ is approximately \blank{2.5cm}.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{2.} The average rate of change of a function $g$ from $x = 0$ to $x = 3$ is
$-4$. In a complete sentence, describe what this tells you about how $g$'s output changes
as $x$ increases from 0 to 3.
