\item Let $f(x) = x^2 + 1$. Find the average rate of change of $f$ over
      $[1, 3]$.

\begin{work}
  \text{AROC} &= \frac{f(3) - f(1)}{3 - 1} \\
              &= \frac{10 - 2}{2} \\
              &= 4
\end{work}

Answer: \ans{4}

  \item The hiker did not stand still. Say in one sentence what the
