\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can determine the average rate of change of a linear function over any interval and explain why it is always the same.
  \item I can compute successive average rates of change for a quadratic function and identify the linear pattern they form.
  \item I can use the pattern of average rates of change to determine whether a function's graph is concave up or concave down.
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
