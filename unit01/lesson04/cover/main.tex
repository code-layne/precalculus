\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can identify the degree, leading term, and leading coefficient of a polynomial function and use them to describe end behavior.
  \item I can locate and classify local and global maxima and minima of a polynomial function.
  \item I can identify points of inflection as places where the rate of change switches from increasing to decreasing (or vice versa).
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
