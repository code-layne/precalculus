\begin{notesbox}{Review: Polynomial Basics --- Key}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=8pt]

\item Given $p(x) = -2x^3 + 5x^2 - x + 3$, identify each of the following:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=6pt]
  \item[(a)] Degree: \ans{3}

  \item[(b)] Leading coefficient: \ans{$-2$}

  \item[(c)] Leading term: \ans{$-2x^3$}
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{4pt}

\item A polynomial function has values $p(-1) = 0$, $p(0) = -3$, $p(2) = 0$, $p(3) = 4$, and $p(4) = 0$.

Between which pairs of consecutive zeros must there be a local maximum or local minimum? Circle all that apply and give a brief reason.

\vspace{4pt}
\hspace{1em} \ans{Between $x = -1$ and $x = 2$} \hfill \ans{Between $x = 2$ and $x = 4$} \hspace{1em}

\vspace{6pt}
Reason: \ans{By EK 1.4.A.3, between every two distinct real zeros of a nonconstant polynomial there must be at least one local maximum or minimum.}

\vspace{4pt}

\item At a local maximum, is the AROC of the function increasing or decreasing as we approach that point from the left? Explain briefly.

\vspace{4pt}
\ans{Decreasing. Before a local maximum the function is increasing (positive AROC), and after it is decreasing (negative AROC), so the AROC transitions from positive to negative --- it is decreasing through the local max.}

\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}
