\begin{scenariobox}[Function Makeover Studio]{sky}
\small
A design studio applies transformation rules to ``restyle'' functions. The base function $f$
is defined by the table below. Your team must construct transformed versions, match
transformations to descriptions, and reverse-engineer transformations from data.

\medskip
\textbf{Base function $f$:}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$    & $-2$ & $-1$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ \\
\hline
$f(x)$ & $4$  & $1$  & $0$ & $1$ & $4$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{scenariobox}

\vspace{0.08in}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,
    title=\textbf{Tier R --- Remediate},
    fonttitle=\bfseries, arc=2mm, left=3mm, right=3mm, top=2mm, bottom=2mm]

Three students each applied a different transformation to $f$. Their tables are shown below.
Match each table to the correct description by writing the letter in the blank.

\medskip
\textbf{Descriptions:} \quad
\textbf{A.}\ $g(x) = f(x) - 3$ \qquad
\textbf{B.}\ $g(x) = f(x+2)$ \qquad
\textbf{C.}\ $g(x) = 2f(x)$

\medskip
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$    & $-2$ & $-1$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ \\
\hline
Table 1: $g(x)$ & $1$ & $-2$ & $-3$ & $-2$ & $1$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\quad Matches description: \ans{A}

\medskip
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$    & $-2$ & $-1$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ \\
\hline
Table 2: $g(x)$ & $8$ & $2$ & $0$ & $2$ & $8$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\quad Matches description: \ans{C}

\medskip
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$    & $-2$ & $-1$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ \\
\hline
Table 3: $g(x)$ & $0$ & $1$ & $4$ & $9$ & $16$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\quad Matches description: \ans{B}

\begin{teachernote}
Table 1: $f(x)-3$ subtracts 3 from each output ($4-3=1$, $1-3=-2$, $0-3=-3$, etc.). \\
Table 2: $2f(x)$ doubles each output ($2\cdot4=8$, $2\cdot1=2$, etc.). \\
Table 3: $f(x+2)$ shifts input left 2, so $g(-2)=f(0)=0$, $g(-1)=f(1)=1$, $g(0)=f(2)=4$, etc.
\end{teachernote}

\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{0.08in}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,
    title=\textbf{Tier A --- Apply},
    fonttitle=\bfseries, arc=2mm, left=3mm, right=3mm, top=2mm, bottom=2mm]

Using the base function $f$ from the scenario, construct the table of values for
$g(x) = 2f(x - 1) + 3$. Show how you compute each output.

\medskip
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.6}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$ & $x-1$ & $f(x-1)$ & $2f(x-1)$ & $g(x) = 2f(x-1)+3$ \\
\hline
$-1$ & \ans{$-2$} & \ans{$4$} & \ans{$8$}  & \ans{$11$} \\
\hline
$0$  & \ans{$-1$} & \ans{$1$} & \ans{$2$}  & \ans{$5$}  \\
\hline
$1$  & \ans{$0$}  & \ans{$0$} & \ans{$0$}  & \ans{$3$}  \\
\hline
$2$  & \ans{$1$}  & \ans{$1$} & \ans{$2$}  & \ans{$5$}  \\
\hline
$3$  & \ans{$2$}  & \ans{$4$} & \ans{$8$}  & \ans{$11$} \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\medskip
State the domain and range of $g$ based on your table.

\medskip
Domain of $g$: \ans{$\{-1,0,1,2,3\}$} \quad Range of $g$: \ans{$\{3,5,11\}$}

\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{0.08in}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,
    title=\textbf{Tier E --- Extend},
    fonttitle=\bfseries, arc=2mm, left=3mm, right=3mm, top=2mm, bottom=2mm]

A second function $h$ is defined by the table below. By comparing $h$ to the base function
$f$, identify all transformations applied to $f$ to produce $h$, and write $h(x)$ in terms
of $f$.

\medskip
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$x$    & $-2$ & $-1$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ \\
\hline
$f(x)$ & $4$  & $1$  & $0$ & $1$ & $4$ \\
\hline
$h(x)$ & $-7$ & $-1$ & $1$ & $-1$ & $-7$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=8pt]
  \item Compare outputs: how does $h(x)$ relate to $f(x)$ at each $x$-value?

        \vspace{0.05in}
        \ansline{$h(x) = -f(x)+1$ at each $x$: e.g. $h(-2)=-4+1=-7$, $h(-1)=-1+1=0$\ldots wait, $h(-1)=-1$. Check: $-f(-1)+1=-1+1=0\ne-1$. Try $h(x)=-f(x)-1$: $h(-2)=-4-1=-5\ne-7$. Try $a=-2$, $k=1$: $-2(4)+1=-7$\checkmark, $-2(1)+1=-1$\checkmark, $-2(0)+1=1$\checkmark.}

  \item Write $h(x)$ in terms of $f(x)$. Identify $a$ and $k$ in $g(x) = af(x)+k$.

        \vspace{0.05in}
        $h(x) = $ \ans{$-2f(x)+1$}, so $a = $ \ans{$-2$} and $k = $ \ans{$1$}

  \item Describe in words all transformations that map $f$ onto $h$.

        \vspace{0.05in}
        \ansline{Vertical stretch by factor 2, reflection over the $x$-axis, then vertical shift up 1 unit.}
\end{enumerate}

\end{tcolorbox}
