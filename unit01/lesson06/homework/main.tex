\begin{reflectionbox}
\textbf{Lesson Preview.} In Lesson 1.7, we study \textit{rational functions} --- ratios of polynomials.
End behavior takes a new form: instead of going to $\pm\infty$, rational functions can approach
finite horizontal asymptotes. Think about what happens when you divide a polynomial by another
polynomial of the same or higher degree.
\end{reflectionbox}
