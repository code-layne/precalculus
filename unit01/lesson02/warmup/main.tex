\textbf{1.} The table below shows selected values of a function $f$.

\vspace{4pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabular}{|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|}
\hline
\rowcolor{sky}
$x$    & 1  & 3  & 6  & 10 \\
\hline
$f(x)$ & 4  & 10 & 19 & 31 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{6pt}
Compute the slope of the line through the points $(1,\,f(1))$ and $(6,\,f(6))$.
Show your work using the slope formula.

\writeline

\writeline

\textbf{2.} A car travels at 55 mph for 2 hours, then at 70 mph for 1 hour.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=3pt]
    \item What is the total distance traveled?
          \writeline
    \item What is the car's average speed over the entire 3-hour trip?
          (Hint: average speed $=$ total distance $\div$ total time.)
          \writeline
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{8pt}

\textbf{3.} The table below gives output values of a function $g$.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tabular}{|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|C{1.6cm}|}
\hline
\rowcolor{sky}
$x$    & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\
\hline
$g(x)$ & 8 & 5 & 3 & 3 & 6 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{6pt}
Based on the table, on what interval(s) is $g$ \textbf{increasing}?
On what interval(s) is $g$ \textbf{decreasing}? (Recall from Lesson~1.1.)
