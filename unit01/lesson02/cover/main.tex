\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can compute the average rate of change of a function over an interval and
        interpret its meaning in context.
  \item I can approximate the rate of change of a function at a point using average
        rates of change over small intervals.
  \item I can use the sign of the rate of change to describe how two quantities vary
        together.
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
