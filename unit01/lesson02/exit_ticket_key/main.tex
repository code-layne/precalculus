\item The table shows how many players are in an online game $t$ hours after
      launch.

\smallskip
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$t$ (hr) & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 \\
\hline
$P(t)$ & 0 & 10 & 18 & 24 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\smallskip

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=4pt]
  \item Circle one: $P$ is \ans{increasing} / \textbf{decreasing}.
  \item Circle one: $P$ is \textbf{concave up} / \ans{concave down}.
  \item Defend your answer to (b) in one sentence, using the words
        \textit{rate of change}.

        \vspace{0.02in}
        \ansline{The gains are 10, 8, 6 --- the rate of change is decreasing, so the graph is concave down.}
\end{enumerate}

\item Complete the definition: \textbf{concave up} means the rate of change
      is \ans{increasing} on that interval.

\item A pond is draining. The table shows the water depth $d(t)$ in inches
      after $t$ days.

\smallskip
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$t$ (days) & 0 & 2 & 4 & 6 \\
\hline
$d(t)$ (in) & 18 & 12 & 6 & 0 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\smallskip

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=4pt]
  \item The zero of $d$ is $t = $ \ans{6}.
  \item What does that zero mean in this context?

        \vspace{0.02in}
        \ansline{Six days after it starts draining, the pond is empty (depth 0 inches).}
\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
