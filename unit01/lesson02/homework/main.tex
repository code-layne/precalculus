\textbf{5.} (AP-Style) The table below gives values of a function $q$.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tabular}{|C{1.5cm}|C{1.5cm}|C{1.5cm}|C{1.5cm}|C{1.5cm}|}
\hline
\rowcolor{sky}
$x$    & 1 & 3 & 5 & 9 \\
\hline
$q(x)$ & 8 & 2 & 6 & 10 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{6pt}
Which of the following best describes the AROC of $q$ over $[1,\,9]$?

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*), itemsep=3pt]
    \item The AROC is $\dfrac{10-8}{9-1} = 0.25$ and is positive.
    \item The AROC is $\dfrac{8-10}{9-1} = -0.25$ and is negative.
    \item The AROC is 2 because $q$ increases by 2 on average.
    \item The AROC cannot be determined from this table.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{extensionbox}
\textbf{Extension (optional):} Let $f(x) = x^2$. Compute the AROC of $f$ over the
interval $[2,\,2+h]$ for $h \neq 0$.

\[
    \text{AROC} = \frac{f(2+h) - f(2)}{h}
    = \frac{(2+h)^2 - 4}{h}
    = \frac{4 + 4h + h^2 - 4}{h}
    = \frac{4h + h^2}{h}
    = \hphantom{000}
\]

What value does this AROC approach as $h$ gets closer and closer to 0?

\writeline
\end{extensionbox}
