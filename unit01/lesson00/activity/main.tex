\boxguard
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,
    title=\textbf{Tier A --- Approaching Proficiency},
    fonttitle=\bfseries, arc=2mm, left=3mm, right=3mm, top=2mm, bottom=2mm]

A phone's battery percentage is recorded during one day.

\smallskip
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
Time & 7 am & 9 am & 11 am & 1 pm & 3 pm & 5 pm \\
\hline
Battery \% & 100 & 80 & 60 & 95 & 75 & 55 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\smallskip

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=8pt]
  \item From 7 am to 11 am the battery percentage is \blank{2.8cm};
        from 11 am to 1 pm it is \blank{2.8cm};
        from 1 pm to 5 pm it is \blank{2.8cm}.

  \item What probably happened between 11 am and 1 pm?

        \vspace{0.05in}
        \writeline

  \item Input quantity: \blank{2.8cm} \quad Output quantity: \blank{3.2cm}

  \item At any single moment, can this phone show two different battery
        percentages? What does your answer tell you about whether battery
        percentage is a \textbf{function} of time?

        \vspace{0.05in}
        \writelines{2}
\end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}

\boxguard
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,

A runner leaves the starting line, jogs to a bench, sits for a water break, then
jogs back to the start. ``Distance'' below means distance from the starting line.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=8pt]
  \item Complete the speed column.

        \smallskip
        \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
        \begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|}
        \hline
        \textbf{Interval} & \textbf{Distance is\ldots} & \textbf{Speed: zero or positive?} \\
        \hline
        start $\to$ bench & increasing & \blank{2.4cm} \\
        \hline
        resting at the bench & constant & \blank{2.4cm} \\
        \hline
        bench $\to$ start & decreasing & \blank{2.4cm} \\
        \hline
        \end{tabular}

  \item The runner is 0.3 km from the start at one moment on the way out
        \textit{and} at one moment on the way back. Two times share the same
        distance --- does that break the function rule for distance as a function
        of time? Explain.

        \vspace{0.05in}
        \writelines{2}

  \item Flip it: is \textbf{time} a function of \textbf{distance} for this runner?
        Explain using the 0.3 km moment.

        \vspace{0.05in}
        \writelines{2}
\end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}
