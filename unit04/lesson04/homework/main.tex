      Rewrite each expression as a single base raised to the $x$ power. Show work.

      \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=10pt]
        \item $f(x) = 3^{4x}$

              \vspace{0.06in}

        \item $g(x) = 25^{x/2}$

              \vspace{0.06in}
              $25^{x/2} = (25^{\blank{0.8cm}})^x = $ \smash{\gradedblank[id=hw2b, type=short, points=1, answer={5^x}]{3cm}{13pt}}
      \end{enumerate}

      Show that $f(x) = 4^{x+3/2}$ and $g(x) = 8 \cdot 4^x \cdot \sqrt{4}^{\,-1}$ are the same
