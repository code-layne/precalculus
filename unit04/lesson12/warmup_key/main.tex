\textbf{2.}\quad A function $f$ satisfies $f(0) = 5$.
Can $f$ be a linear transformation?  Explain your reasoning.

\vspace{0.08in}
\ans{No. A linear transformation must map the zero vector to the zero vector ($L(\vec{0})=\vec{0}$). Since $f(0)=5\ne 0$, $f$ cannot be a linear transformation.}

\vspace{0.10in}

\textbf{3.}\quad Express the point $(-2,\, 5)$ as a $2 \times 1$ column matrix.

\vspace{0.08in}
\hspace{1em} \ans{$\begin{bmatrix}-2 \\ 5\end{bmatrix}$}
