\begin{notesbox}{Vector-Valued Functions Check}

A particle moves with position vector $\vec{p}(t)=\langle t^{2}-1,\;2t+3\rangle$
(distances in meters, $t$ in seconds).

\vspace{10pt}
\textbf{1.}\quad Find $\vec{p}(2)$.  What does this vector represent in context?

\vspace{8pt}
$\vec{p}(2) = \langle \blank{3.5cm}\rangle$

\vspace{6pt}
In context, $\vec{p}(2)$ represents: \writeline

\vspace{14pt}
\textbf{2.}\quad Find $\|\vec{p}(2)\|$ to the nearest tenth of a meter.

\vspace{8pt}
\hspace{1em} $\|\vec{p}(2)\| = \sqrt{\blank{1.8cm}^{2}+\blank{1.8cm}^{2}}
= \sqrt{\blank{2.5cm}} \approx $ \blank{2cm} m

\vspace{14pt}
\textbf{3.}\quad The velocity vector is $\vec{v}(t)=\langle 2t,\;2\rangle$.
Find the speed at $t=1$.

\vspace{8pt}
\hspace{1em} $\vec{v}(1) = \langle \blank{3cm}\rangle$ \qquad
Speed $= \sqrt{\blank{2.5cm}} = $ \blank{3cm}
