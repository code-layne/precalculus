% ---- Problem 5: Real-world context ----
\item A nutrition label lists the amounts of Protein (P), Fat (F), and Carbohydrates (C)
per gram for two foods:
\[
  N = \begin{bmatrix}0.25 & 0.10 & 0.60 \\ 0.30 & 0.05 & 0.55\end{bmatrix}
  \quad \text{(rows = Food 1, Food 2; cols = P, F, C)}
\]
A serving consists of $\mathbf{s} = \begin{bmatrix}200 \\ 150\end{bmatrix}$ grams
(Food~1 and Food~2 respectively).

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=6pt]
  \item Can you compute $N\mathbf{s}$? State the dimensions of $N$ and $\mathbf{s}$
        and check compatibility.
  \item Compute $N\mathbf{s}$. Each entry represents the total grams of one nutrient.
        Identify what Protein, Fat, and Carbohydrate totals are.
\end{enumerate}
