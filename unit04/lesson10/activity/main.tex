Is $A\mathbf{v}$ defined? \underline{\hspace{2cm}}

\medskip

\medskip

$= 1(\phantom{0}) + 2(\phantom{0}) = $ \underline{\hspace{1.5cm}}

\medskip
Entry $(2,1)$: \textit{row 2 of $A$} $\cdot$ \textit{col 1 of $\mathbf{v}$}
$= 3(\phantom{0}) + 4(\phantom{0}) = $ \underline{\hspace{1.5cm}}

\medskip
$A\mathbf{v} = \begin{bmatrix}\phantom{00} \\ \phantom{00}\end{bmatrix}$

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,
    title=\textbf{Tier E --- Extend: Revenue Matrix},
    fonttitle=\bfseries, arc=2mm, left=3mm, right=3mm, top=2mm, bottom=2mm]

A store sells 3 items priced at \$10, \$5, and \$8. The row-price vector is
$p = \begin{bmatrix}10 & 5 & 8\end{bmatrix}$.

Units sold on two days are recorded in the matrix
$Q = \begin{bmatrix}3 & 1 \\ 2 & 4 \\ 0 & 3\end{bmatrix}$,
where column 1 is Day~1 and column 2 is Day~2.

\medskip
\textbf{(a)} What are the dimensions of $p$? \underline{\hspace{2cm}}
What are the dimensions of $Q$? \underline{\hspace{2cm}}
Is $pQ$ defined? \underline{\hspace{1.5cm}}
What will be the dimensions of $pQ$? \underline{\hspace{2cm}}

\medskip
\textbf{(b)} Compute $pQ$.
\vspace{1.6in}

\textbf{(c)} Interpret each entry of $pQ$ in the context of the problem.
\vspace{0.8in}

\end{tcolorbox}
