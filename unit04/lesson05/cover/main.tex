\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can graph an equation in two variables by finding solutions and identifying the
        explicit function pieces it describes.
  \item I can determine whether two variables in an implicit equation co-vary positively or
        negatively by examining the sign of the ratio of their changes.
  \item I can identify where the rate of change is zero or undefined and interpret this
        geometrically (horizontal or vertical behavior).
\end{enumerate}
