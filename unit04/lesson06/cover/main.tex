\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can write the standard form equation of a parabola given its vertex
    and one other point, for both vertical-axis and horizontal-axis orientations.
  \item I can write the standard form equation of an ellipse (including circles)
    given its center, horizontal radius, and vertical radius.
  \item I can write the standard form equation of a hyperbola and identify its
    center, orientation, and asymptote equations.
