\item \textbf{(AP-Style Multiple Choice)} A student fits three models to a data set and records
      the following:

      \begin{center}
      \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
      \begin{tabular}{l|c|c}
      \textbf{Model} & \textbf{$R^2$} & \textbf{Residual plot} \\ \hline
      Linear      & 0.94 & clear upward curve \\
      Quadratic   & 0.97 & slight curve \\
      Exponential & 0.99 & no pattern \\
      \end{tabular}
      \end{center}

      Which model should the student choose?

      \medskip
      \begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*), itemsep=3pt]
        \item Linear, because it has the simplest equation.
        \item Quadratic, because its $R^2$ is higher than linear.
        \item Exponential, because it has the highest $R^2$ and a patternless residual plot.
        \item Quadratic, because its residual plot has a smaller curve than linear's.
      \end{enumerate}

      \vspace{0.04in}
      Answer: \blank{1.5cm}
