\begin{scenariobox}[Linear or Exponential?]{plumlight}
\small
Every function we study today is either linear or exponential --- but they don't come with labels.
Your job is to investigate tables and pairs of points, apply the equal-length interval test,
and build the function formula. Work in your assigned tier. Be ready to share your reasoning.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,
    title=\textbf{Tier A --- Apply (Key)},
    fonttitle=\bfseries, arc=2mm, left=3mm, right=3mm, top=2mm, bottom=2mm]

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=10pt]
  \item Two points: $(1,\ 6)$ and $(3,\ 54)$.

        Ratio test: $\dfrac{54}{6} = $ \ans{9} over $3 - 1 = 2$ input units,
        so $r = $ \ans{$\sqrt{9} = 3$}.

        $f(x) = $ \ans{$6 \cdot 3^{x-1}$} \quad Simplified: $f(x) = $ \ans{$2 \cdot 3^x$}

        \textit{Check: $f(1) = 2 \cdot 3 = 6$\ \checkmark\quad $f(3) = 2 \cdot 27 = 54$\ \checkmark}

  \item Two points: $(2,\ 7)$ and $(5,\ 16)$.

        Slope: $m = \dfrac{16 - 7}{5 - 2} = $ \ans{3}

        $g(x) = $ \ans{$7 + 3(x - 2)$} \quad Simplified: $g(x) = $ \ans{$3x + 1$}

        \textit{Check: $g(2) = 7$\ \checkmark\quad $g(5) = 16$\ \checkmark}

  \item Candle: $h(t)$, two points $(2, 14)$ and $(5, 8)$.

        $m = \dfrac{8 - 14}{5 - 2} = -2$. \quad $h(t) = $ \ans{$14 - 2(t-2) = -2t + 18$}

        Set $h(t) = 2$: $-2t + 18 = 2 \Rightarrow t = $ \ans{8 hours}
\end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,
    title=\textbf{Tier E --- Extend (Key)},
    fonttitle=\bfseries, arc=2mm, left=3mm, right=3mm, top=2mm, bottom=2mm]

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=10pt]
  \item Direct interval test on $P$: differences are $4, 12, 36$ (not constant); ratios are
        $26/22 \approx 1.18,\ 38/26 \approx 1.46,\ 74/38 \approx 1.95$ (not constant).
        \ans{Neither purely linear nor purely exponential.}

  \item $P - 20$ column: \ans{2, 6, 18, 54}.

        Differences: $4, 12, 36$ (not constant).
        Ratios: \ans{3, 3, 3} --- \ans{Yes}, constant ratio $= 3$.

  \item $a = 2$, $b = 3$, so $P - 20 = 2 \cdot 3^t$.

        $P(t) = $ \ans{$2 \cdot 3^t + 20$}

  \item \ans{The population is not growing by a pure exponential pattern; there is a background
        (baseline) of 20 individuals that remains constant, and the additional population above
        that baseline triples each year. This ``exponential plus a constant'' form means the
        overall growth rate appears to slow down at first glance but the underlying exponential
        growth is revealed by subtracting the constant.}
\end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}
