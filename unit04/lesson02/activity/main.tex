\begin{scenariobox}[Remote-Control Car]{sky}
A remote-control car moves along a surface. Its position in meters at
time $t$ seconds is given by the parametric function
\[
  f(t) = \bigl(x(t),\; y(t)\bigr) = \bigl(t^2 - 6t + 5,\;
  {-t^2 + 4t}\bigr), \quad 0 \le t \le 6,
\]
where $x(t)$ measures meters east of the starting line and $y(t)$
measures meters north.
\end{scenariobox}

\begin{notesbox}{Part 2 --- Analyzing the Car's Motion}

\begin{notesbox}{Part 3 --- Extension}

\textbf{7.}\quad Consider the parametric function
$g(t) = (\cos t,\; \sin t)$ for $0 \le t \le 2\pi$.

\vspace{4pt}
(a)\quad What are the maximum and minimum values of $x(t) = \cos t$?
What are the horizontal extrema of this curve?
\writelines{2}

\vspace{0.04in}
(b)\quad What are the maximum and minimum values of $y(t) = \sin t$?
What are the vertical extrema?
\writelines{2}

\vspace{0.04in}
(c)\quad Find all values of $t$ in $[0, 2\pi]$ where $x(t) = 0$.
What are the $y$-intercepts of the curve?
\writelines{2}
