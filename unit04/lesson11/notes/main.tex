\begin{notesbox}{The Inverse of a Matrix}
The \textbf{inverse} of matrix $A$ is the matrix $A^{-1}$ such that:
\[
    A \cdot A^{-1} = \blank{2cm} \qquad \text{and} \qquad A^{-1} \cdot A = \blank{2cm}
\]

\textbf{Inverse formula for a $2\times2$ matrix:} If $A = \begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}$, then
\[
    A^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{\blank{2cm}}\begin{bmatrix}\blank{0.8cm} & \blank{0.8cm} \\[8pt]
    \blank{0.8cm} & \blank{0.8cm}\end{bmatrix}
\]

\textbf{Example:} Find $A^{-1}$ for $A = \begin{bmatrix}3&2\\1&4\end{bmatrix}$.
\vspace{0.7in}
\end{notesbox}
