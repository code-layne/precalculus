\begin{hookbox}
\textit{A school cafeteria offers two lunch choices every day: \textbf{Pizza (P)} and \textbf{Salad (S)}.
On Day~0, \textbf{100 students} choose Pizza and \textbf{80 students} choose Salad.
Each day, 70\% of Pizza choosers pick Pizza again (30\% switch to Salad), and 60\% of Salad choosers
pick Salad again (40\% switch to Pizza).}

\vspace{6pt}
Prediction --- after many days, approximately how many students will choose each option?

\writeline
\end{hookbox}

\vspace{0.06in}

\begin{notesbox}{Building the Transition Matrix}

Each \textbf{column} describes what fraction of members of that state go to each state next.

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l|cc}
 & \textbf{From P} & \textbf{From S} \\\hline
\textbf{To P} & \blank{1.4cm} & \blank{1.4cm} \\[4pt]
\textbf{To S} & \blank{1.4cm} & \blank{1.4cm} \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{8pt}
$T = \begin{bmatrix}\blank{1.5cm} & \blank{1.5cm} \\ \blank{1.5cm} & \blank{1.5cm}\end{bmatrix}$
\qquad Each column sums to \blank{1cm}.

\vspace{6pt}
Initial state vector: $\vec{s}_0 = \begin{bmatrix}100 \\ 80\end{bmatrix}$

\end{notesbox}

\vspace{0.06in}

\begin{notesbox}{Predicting Future States}

To find the \textbf{next} state vector, multiply: \quad next state $= $ \blank{1.5cm} $\times$ \blank{1.5cm}

\vspace{8pt}
$\vec{s}_1 = T\vec{s}_0
= \begin{bmatrix}0.7(\phantom{000})+0.4(\phantom{000}) \\ 0.3(\phantom{000})+0.6(\phantom{000})\end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix}\blank{2cm} \\ \blank{2cm}\end{bmatrix}$

\vspace{10pt}
$\vec{s}_2 = T\vec{s}_1
= \begin{bmatrix}\blank{5cm} \\ \blank{5cm}\end{bmatrix}$

\end{notesbox}

\vspace{0.06in}

\begin{notesbox}{The Steady State}

A \textbf{steady state} $\vec{s}_\infty$ satisfies: $T\vec{s}_\infty = $ \blank{2cm}

\vspace{6pt}
Repeated multiplication converges: the state vectors approach a fixed vector that never changes.

\vspace{6pt}
What pattern do you notice as you compute more state vectors? \writeline

\end{notesbox}

\vspace{0.06in}

\begin{notesbox}{Finding Past States}

Since $\vec{s}_1 = T\vec{s}_0$, we can recover $\vec{s}_0$ by multiplying by the \textbf{inverse}:

\vspace{6pt}
$\vec{s}_{n-1} = $ \blank{2cm} $\cdot\, \vec{s}_n$

\vspace{8pt}
Apply to find Day~0 from Day~1: $T^{-1}\vec{s}_1 = $ \blank{5cm}

\vspace{6pt}

\end{notesbox}
