\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can find the component form and magnitude of a vector given its tail
    and head, and extract components from a vector described by magnitude and
    direction angle using trigonometry. \textit{(LO 4.8.A)}
  \item I can perform scalar multiplication and vector addition algebraically
    and interpret each operation geometrically; I can compute the dot product
    of two vectors. \textit{(LO 4.8.B)}
  \item I can find the unit vector in the direction of a given vector and express
    any vector in terms of $\hat{\imath}$ and $\hat{\jmath}$. \textit{(LO 4.8.C)}
  \item I can use the dot product formula to find the angle between two vectors
    and determine whether two vectors are perpendicular. \textit{(LO 4.8.D)}
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
