\begin{notesbox}{Parametrically Defined Circles and Lines Practice}

\textbf{1.}\quad A circular fountain has radius 6 m, centered at the
point $(-1,\,3)$. A water jet starts at $(-1+6,\,3) = (5,\,3)$ and
rotates counterclockwise.

\vspace{4pt}
(a)\quad Write parametric equations for the jet's position at time $t$.

\writeline

(b)\quad Find the jet's position when $t = \pi/2$ and when $t = \pi$.
\writelines{2}

\vspace{0.08in}
\textbf{2.}\quad Consider the circular path
$x(t) = -2 + 5\cos t$, $y(t) = 4 + 5\sin t$, $0 \le t \le 2\pi$.

\vspace{4pt}
(a)\quad State the center and radius of the circle.
\writeline

(b)\quad Find all values of $t$ in the domain where $x(t) = -2$.
What are the coordinates of the circle at those values?
\writelines{3}

\vspace{0.08in}
\textbf{3.}\quad Write the parametric equations for a \emph{clockwise}
circle of radius 7 centered at $(2,\,-3)$.

\writeline

At $t = \pi/2$, where is the point? Show the computation.
\writelines{2}

\vspace{0.08in}
\textbf{4.}\quad A hiker walks from campsite $A = (1,\,4)$ to
viewpoint $B = (9,\,0)$ in a straight line.

\vspace{4pt}
(a)\quad Write parametric equations for the hiker's position
for $0 \le t \le 1$.
\writeline

(b)\quad Find the hiker's position at $t = 0.25$ and at $t = 0.75$.
\writelines{2}

(c)\quad At what parameter value $t$ does the hiker's path cross
the $x$-axis (where $y = 0$)? Show your algebra.
\writelines{3}

\end{notesbox}

\vspace{0.08in}

\begin{notesbox}{AP-Style Practice}

\textbf{5.}\quad The parametric equations $x(t) = 3 + 4\cos t$ and
$y(t) = -2 + 4\sin t$, $0 \le t \le 2\pi$, describe the position of
a point moving along a circle. Which of the following is the
$y$-coordinate of the point when $x(t) = 3$?

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item $2$
  \item $-6$
  \item $2$ and $-6$
  \item $-2$
\end{enumerate}

\end{notesbox}

\vspace{0.08in}
