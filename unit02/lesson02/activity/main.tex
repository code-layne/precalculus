\begin{scenariobox}[Linear or Exponential?]{sky}
\small
Every function we study today is either linear or exponential --- but they don't come with labels.
Your job is to investigate tables and pairs of points, apply the equal-length interval test,
and build the function formula. Work in your assigned tier. Be ready to share your reasoning.
\end{scenariobox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black!40,
    title=\textbf{Tier A --- Apply},
    fonttitle=\bfseries, arc=2mm, left=3mm, right=3mm, top=2mm, bottom=2mm]

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=10pt]
  \item Two points: $(1,\ 6)$ and $(3,\ 54)$. Test whether the relationship is exponential.

        \vspace{0.06in}
        Ratio test: $\dfrac{54}{6} = \blank{1.5cm}$ over $3 - 1 = 2$ input units,
        so $r = \blank{2.5cm}$.

        Write the exponential function: $f(x) = \blank{2cm} \cdot \blank{2cm}^{x - \blank{1cm}}$

        \vspace{0.04in}
        Simplify to $f(x) = ab^x$: $f(x) = $ \blank{5cm}

  \item Two points: $(2,\ 7)$ and $(5,\ 16)$. Test whether the relationship is linear.

        \vspace{0.06in}
        Slope: $m = \dfrac{16 - 7}{5 - 2} = \blank{2cm}$

        Write the linear function: $g(x) = \blank{2cm} + \blank{2cm}(x - \blank{1cm})$

        \vspace{0.04in}
        Simplify to $g(x) = b + mx$: $g(x) = $ \blank{5cm}

  \item A candle burns down at a constant rate. After 2 hours it is 14 cm tall; after 5 hours it
        is 8 cm tall. Write a linear function $h(t)$ for the height (cm) at time $t$ (hours).
        After how many hours will the candle be 2 cm tall?

        \vspace{0.06in}
        $h(t) = $ \blank{5cm} \quad Reaches 2 cm when $t = $ \blank{2.5cm}
\end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}

        Apply the equal-length interval test directly to $P$. Is it linear? Exponential?

        \vspace{0.06in}
        \writelines{2}

  \item Now complete the $P - 20$ column above. Apply the interval test to $P - 20$.

        \vspace{0.06in}
        First differences of $P - 20$: \blank{5cm}

        Ratios of $P - 20$: \blank{5cm} \quad Constant ratio? \blank{2cm}

  \item If $P - 20 = a \cdot b^t$, find $a$ and $b$, then write $P(t)$.

        \vspace{0.06in}
        $a = $ \blank{2cm}, \quad $b = $ \blank{2cm}

        $P(t) = $ \blank{6cm}

  \item In a complete sentence, explain what it means that the population follows an
        ``exponential-plus-constant'' pattern rather than a pure exponential pattern.

        \vspace{0.06in}
        \writelines{2}
\end{enumerate}
