\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can read the intervals where a polynomial function is increasing or
        decreasing from a pre-drawn graph or from a table of values.
  \item I can identify local (relative) maxima and minima --- including at an
        included endpoint of a restricted domain --- and decide which of them
        are the global (absolute) maximum and minimum.
  \item I can explain why there must be a local maximum or minimum between
        two distinct real zeros, and why an even-degree polynomial has a
        global maximum or a global minimum while an odd-degree one has
        neither.
  \item I can locate a point of inflection as the input where the
        \textit{rate of change} turns around, and tell that apart from where
        the function itself turns around.
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
