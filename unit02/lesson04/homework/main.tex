\item A polynomial $p$ has real coefficients, degree 3, leading coefficient 1,
      and zeros $x = 5$ and $x = 1 - 2i$.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=6pt]
  \item The third zero is \blank{2.4cm}
  \item Write $p(x)$ in expanded form.

        \begin{work}
          p(x) &= (x-5)\big((x-1) + 2i\big)\big((x-1) - 2i\big) \\
               &= (x-5)\big((x-1)^2 - 4i^2\big) \\
               &= (x-5)\big(x^2 - 2x + 1 + 4\big) \\
               &= (x-5)(x^2 - 2x + 5) \\
               &= x^3 - 7x^2 + 15x - 25
        \end{work}

        $p(x) = $ \blank{6cm}
\end{enumerate}

\smallskip
Now verify your answer to (b) by substituting $-x$.

\begin{work}
  g(-x) &= 2(-x)^5 - (-x)^3 \\
        &= -2x^5 + x^3 \\
        &= -\big(2x^5 - x^3\big) \\
        &= -g(x)
\end{work}

So $g$ is \blank{2cm}.
