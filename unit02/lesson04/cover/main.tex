\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can identify the zeros of a polynomial from its factored form and state their
        multiplicities.
  \item I can explain whether a polynomial's graph crosses or is tangent to the $x$-axis
        at each real zero based on the multiplicity.
  \item I can determine whether a polynomial function is even, odd, or neither using the
        algebraic test $f(-x)=f(x)$ or $f(-x)=-f(x)$.
  \item I can use complex conjugate pairs to identify all zeros of a polynomial with real
        coefficients.
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
