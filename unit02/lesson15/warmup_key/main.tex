\item Using your values from Question 1, compute $\log_{10}(y)$ for each $x$.
      Round to three decimal places.

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{2.0}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
\rowcolor{sky}
$x$ & $0$ & $1$ & $2$ & $3$ & $4$ \\
\hline

\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{4pt}
What do you notice about how the $\log_{10}(y)$ values change from one $x$ to the next?

\ansline{They increase by approximately $0.301$ each step --- a constant rate of change.
$0.301 \approx \log_{10}(2)$, the base of the exponential.}

\item Using your table from Question 2, what is the slope of the linear relationship
      between $x$ and $\log_{10}(y)$?

\vspace{4pt}
Slope $= $ \ans{$0.301 \approx \log_{10}(2)$}
