\item Evaluate $3x^2 - 5x + 4$ when $x = 2$.

\vspace{0.1in}
Value: \ans{$3(4) - 10 + 4 = 6$}

\item Combine like terms: \quad $4x^2 + 7x - x^2 - 2 + 3x$

\begin{work}
  4x^2 + 7x - x^2 - 2 + 3x &= (4x^2 - x^2) + (7x + 3x) - 2 \\
                           &= 3x^2 + 10x - 2
\end{work}

Simplified: \ans{$3x^2 + 10x - 2$}
