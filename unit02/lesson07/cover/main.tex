\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item I can evaluate $(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$ for given values using tables, formulas, or
        step-by-step substitution, and I understand that the domain of $f \circ g$ is restricted
        to inputs of $g$ whose outputs lie in the domain of $f$.
  \item I can construct the formula for a composite function $f(g(x))$ by substituting $g(x)$
        for every instance of $x$ in $f$, and I can explain why $f \circ g$ and $g \circ f$ are
        generally different functions.
  \item I can decompose a given function into a composition of two simpler functions and connect
        additive and multiplicative transformations to composition.
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
