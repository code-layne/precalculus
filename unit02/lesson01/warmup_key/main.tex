\item From Lesson 1.3: find the slope of the line through $(2, 7)$ and
      $(6, 19)$.

\begin{work}
  m &= \frac{19 - 7}{6 - 2} \\
    &= \frac{12}{4} \\
    &= 3
\end{work}

$m = $ \ans{3}
