\textbf{2.}\quad Two points are plotted.  Compute the slope of the line
through them.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.75]
  \draw[->] (-0.4,0) -- (3.6,0) node[right]{\small $x$};
  \draw[->] (0,-0.4) -- (0,3.6) node[above]{\small $y$};
  \foreach \x in {1,2,3} \draw (\x,0.06)--(\x,-0.06) node[below]{\small $\x$};
  \foreach \y in {1,2,3} \draw (0.06,\y)--(-0.06,\y) node[left]{\small $\y$};
  \fill[navy] (0,0) circle (2.5pt) node[below left]{\small $(0,0)$};
  \fill[navy] (2,3) circle (2.5pt) node[above right]{\small $(2,3)$};
  \draw[navy, thick] (-0.2,-0.3) -- (2.5,3.75);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\vspace{4pt}
Slope $= \dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \dfrac{\ans{3}}{\ans{2}} = $ \ans{$\dfrac{3}{2} = 1.5$}

\textbf{3.}\quad The function $h(x) = \dfrac{1}{x-2}$ is undefined at $x = 2$.

\vspace{4pt}
(a)\quad State the vertical asymptote of $h$: $x = $ \ans{$2$}

\vspace{6pt}
(b)\quad Complete the table to see what happens as $x \to 2^+$.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|}
\hline
$x$ & $h(x)$ \\
\hline
$3$ & \ans{$1$} \\
$2.5$ & \ans{$2$} \\
$2.1$ & \ans{$10$} \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{4pt}
(c)\quad As $x \to 2^+$, the values of $h(x)$ \ans{increase without bound (approach $+\infty$)}
