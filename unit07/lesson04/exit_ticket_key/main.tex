\textbf{1.}\quad Given that $\cos\theta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, find $\sec\theta$.
Show your work.

\vspace{4pt}
$\sec\theta = \dfrac{1}{\cos\theta} = \dfrac{1}{\ans{$-1/2$}} = $ \ans{$-2$}

\vspace{0.18in}
\textbf{2.}\quad The domain of $\csc\theta$ excludes certain values of $\theta$.

\vspace{4pt}
(a)\quad Which trigonometric function is in the denominator of $\csc\theta$?
\ans{sine}

\vspace{6pt}
(b)\quad List the general form of the excluded values:
$\theta = $ \ans{$n\pi$ for integer $n$}

\vspace{0.18in}
\textbf{3.}\quad Which of the following is the period of $f(\theta) = \cot\theta$?

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
(A)\; $2\pi$ \qquad & (B)\; $\pi$ \qquad & (C)\; $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ \qquad & (D)\; $4\pi$
\end{tabular}

\vspace{6pt}
Answer: \ans{(B)}
